Mini Electronics Projects

Welcome to my collection of mini electronics projects, where I explore and experiment with the fundamentals of electronics and embedded systems.

Each project in this folder represents a small hands-on activity — from basic breadboard circuits to simple automation ideas — helping me strengthen my understanding of how electronic components work together.

⸻

🔍 Overview

This repository includes a variety of beginner-friendly projects built using basic components such as transistors, resistors, LEDs, buzzers, and sensors.
Each project focuses on a specific concept, such as switching, indication, or signal detection.

⸻

📁 Repository Contents
	•	🔌 Breadboard Circuits – Simple practical connections and testing
	•	💡 Component Experiments – Projects focusing on specific components
	•	🔔 Indicator Circuits – LED and buzzer-based alert systems
	•	📸 Project Images – Real setup photos and results
	•	🧾 Notes & Learnings – Key takeaways from each experiment

⸻

🎯 Objective

The goal of this repository is to document my learning journey in electronics through small, consistent experiments — building a strong foundation for advanced topics like microcontrollers and embedded systems.

🚀 Future Plans
